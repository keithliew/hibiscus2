# Proposal: "The Lexis Track Record" blog series on hibiscus2.com

Draft for Keith, 21 Oct 2026. Nothing written for the site yet; nothing deployed.

## The idea in one line

Show buyers what Lexis has already built and run, so Lexis Hibiscus 2 reads as the next chapter of a proven resort, not a promise on paper.

The angle is proof of delivery: what was built, when it opened, the records and awards it earned, and how it is run today. It is written for people weighing a villa purchase. Following hibiscus2.com's rules, it says nothing about returns, yields or percentages, and it doesn't link to grrmalaysia.com.

- **Answer-ready FAQ (from verified facts only)**

## Date conflict to settle (for Keith and the grrmalaysia chat)

Both are true, for different events. The sister resort **opened to guests on 1 April 2015** (soft opening, TTG Asia) and was **officially opened on 31 July 2017** (the resort's own news page). The sales kit's "completed 2015" probably refers to the 2015 opening, but no public source confirms a completion date, so say "opened 2015" rather than "completed 2015". Any page that says "opened in 2017" should say "officially opened in July 2017, welcoming guests since April 2015".
