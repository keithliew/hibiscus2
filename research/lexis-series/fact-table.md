## Date conflict (for Keith and the grrmalaysia chat)

Both dates are right, for different events. The resort **soft-opened on 1 April 2015** (TTG Asia; Tourism Malaysia says "opened in 2015"). Its **official opening ceremony was on 31 July 2017** (the resort's own news page). The sales kit's "completed in 2015" matches the soft opening. A page saying it "opened in 2017" should say "officially opened in July 2017, after welcoming guests from April 2015".
