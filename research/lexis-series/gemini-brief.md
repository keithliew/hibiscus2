# Brief: source sweep on Lexis Hibiscus Port Dickson (research only)

You are helping research a blog series for a Malaysian property marketing site about the developer's track record. Do NOT write marketing copy. Use web search and report facts with sources.

## Subject

Lexis Hibiscus Port Dickson (Negeri Sembilan, Malaysia), the existing overwater-villa resort built by the KL Metro Group / Kuala Lumpur Metro Group and operated by Lexis Hotel Group. (A newer project, Lexis Hibiscus 2, is being built next to it; only mention it if a source discusses it.)

## Questions to answer, each with source URLs

1. Opening and completion dates. When was construction completed, and when did the resort open to guests (soft and official opening)? Sources may disagree (2015 vs 2016 vs 2017): list each date with its source and say which is "completion" and which is "opening".
2. Guinness World Records: the exact record titles, the number held, the date verified, and what was counted (for example the number of pools). Prefer guinnessworldrecords.com.
3. Size: number of villas/rooms, number of private pools, land or sea area, and the hibiscus-flower layout (who designed it, why that shape).
4. Awards won by Lexis Hibiscus (name of award, category, year, awarding body). Up to 15, most notable first.
5. Developer and operator history: KL Metro Group founding year, founders if public, other Lexis properties (Lexis Port Dickson, Grand Lexis Port Dickson, Lexis Suites Penang, Imperial Lexis Kuala Lumpur) with opening years.
6. Notable features and firsts: for example overwater villas on the Straits of Malacca, Jacuzzi/pool per villa, sea-water/salt-water details, restaurants, Hibiscus Walk, environmental or engineering notes (piling, sea works).
7. Reputation signals that are public and citable: news coverage (The Star, NST, The Edge, Malay Mail, travel media), tourism ministry mentions, review-site rating summaries (TripAdvisor/Google rating and review count only, no review text).
8. Anything negative or disputed that a careful writer should know about (incidents, controversies, delays), with sources.

## Rules

- Every fact must have at least one URL. Mark each fact HIGH (official or primary source), MEDIUM (reputable news), or LOW (blog, forum, aggregator).
- Do not include return, yield, rental percentage or investment figures; they are out of scope. If a source focuses on them, list only its URL under "skipped".
- If you cannot find something, say "not found". Do not guess.

## Reply shape

A markdown table per question: fact | value | source URL | confidence | date of source. Then a short "conflicts" section and a "skipped" list.
