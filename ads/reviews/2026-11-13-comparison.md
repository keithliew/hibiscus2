# hibiscus2 November ads: four-reviewer comparison (CoS, 13 Nov 2026)

Reviewers: ChatGPT (Codex), Fable, Sonnet and Haiku, all given the same brief (`2026-11-13-ad-copy-review-brief.md`). Each full review is in this folder. Nothing in Google Ads was changed.

## Where all four agree
- **No ad except ad 1 says "for sale".** Ads 2–5 can read like hotel or holiday adverts, which matches the first lead being a holiday seeker.
- **Ads 2 and 3 should get one shared rewrite, accepting that the landing-page test restarts.** With 28 clicks and 1 conversion between them, little is lost.
- **Ad 1's low score comes from its two pins.** Removing them would raise the score but lose the buyer filter on the ad that has 4 of the 5 conversions, so keep a filter.
- **Ad 5's "furnished" isn't in approved-facts.md** and should come out.

## Why ad 2 looks strange (combined view)
Google joins two descriptions into one paragraph, and ours break at the join: "…From RM860,000. Run day to day by Lexis Hotel Group…" has no subject. The price and the pool each appear twice. "Day to day … rental" reads like a holiday rental. Nothing says "for sale", and there is no clear ask (Fable, Sonnet, Haiku).

## Where they differ
- **Ad 1 pins.** ChatGPT and Haiku keep the two pins exactly as they are. Fable adds alternatives: 2 lines pinned to position 1 and 3 to position 2, all buyer-filter lines. Sonnet pins 3 lines to position 1 only. **Pick: Fable.** Every ad still says "for sale, not a stay", Google gets more variety, and the expected score is Average.
- **Why ad 2 scores Average while its identical twin, ad 3, scores Excellent.** Fable and ChatGPT both say the live ads must differ (a pin or a missing line). Check this in Google Ads before rewriting.

## Extra findings (verify in Google Ads)
- **Ad 5 has zero impressions.** The campaign-wide negative keyword "hotel" blocks its own keyword "hotel managed villa malaysia" (Fable, Sonnet). The other keywords are probably low-volume. This is a delivery problem, not a copy problem.
- **Ad 4 (Chinese).** "吉隆坡展厅参观" still means the old KL showroom (Sonnet). A native speaker should check the new Chinese lines.
- **Brief check (Haiku).** The landing paths quoted to us (/villas/for-sale, /programme/port-dickson) may be display paths. The plan uses /villas/ and /rental-programme/.
- **Conversions are WhatsApp button taps,** not checked leads.

## Recommended proposal: Fable's rewrite (`2026-11-13-review-fable.md`)
1. **Ads 2 and 3.** First open both side by side in Google Ads and find the difference. Then apply Fable's shared rewrite to both: every description is a standalone sentence carrying a buyer word, and "For sale" appears in headlines 2, 3 and 8.
2. **Ad 1.** Keep the filter. Add the extra pinned buyer lines, change "Book" to "Arrange", and drop the operator and programme headlines, which stay in the descriptions.
3. **Ad 5.** Exempt "hotel managed villa malaysia" from the "hotel" negative, or drop that keyword. Apply Fable's for-sale rewrite and remove "furnished".
4. **Ad 4.** Apply Fable's lines after a native Chinese check.
